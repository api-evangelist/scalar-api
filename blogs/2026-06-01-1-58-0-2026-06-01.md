AsyncAPI support and improved bundle performance This release brings AsyncAPI documents closer to feature parity with OpenAPI. AsyncAPI specs now render schemas as Models, display channels in the content area, and surface introduction headings in the sidebar and search. We also shipped a new ESM standalone bundle with code splitting that reduces the initial load by over 500 KB.
